own it, and the consensus says demand is terminal. The numbers say otherwise:

- the only London-listed analyst figure I found (543.78p) sits *below* the price. Even the analysts are behind.

- CON-5 (negative news in the past week) is technically met by the Eskom outage, but that's sentiment noise, not
  capitulation.
