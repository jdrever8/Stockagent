# Thungela Resources: quality (discretionary)

**Verdict: AVOID.** It fails the quality test, even though it's cheap.

**My test.** A durable high return on capital, pricing power, and a business I'd hold for years (QUA-3 sells only
on a thesis break).

**Where Thungela fails.**
1. **No pricing power.** It's a price-taker in seaborne thermal coal. It even realises 13–16% *below* benchmark.
2. **Returns ride the cycle, not a moat.** H1 profit +467% and EBITDA +91% came from the coal price recovering,
   not from anything the company controls. The same leverage works in reverse: the shares were 319.5p within the
   last year.
3. **A shrinking end-market.** Thermal coal demand faces structural decline, and the shareholder base is narrowed
   by ESG exclusions. That caps the multiple I'd ever be paid on exit.
4. **Concentrated infrastructure risk.** Exports depend on Transnet's rail and Eskom's power. Neither is in
   management's control.

**What I respect.** Capital discipline: R6.1bn net cash, a dividend above policy, cost guidance held, and Ensham's
unit costs down from R1,904 to R1,466/t. If I wanted a cyclical, this is a well-run one.

**Comparison.** My holdings (RELX, Experian, LSEG, Auto Trader, Alphabet) price their own products. Thungela can't.

**Action:** none. Not added to the watchlist.
