# Thungela Resources: macro (discretionary)

**Verdict: BUY, 5% satellite.** It fits the energy-inflation regime.

**Why it fits my book.** My thesis is a stagflationary energy shock: the Fed is hiking, UK inflation is rising on
energy, and Brent is $104. Thermal coal is the same trade one step down the energy stack. When gas and oil spike,
power generators switch to coal.
- **Richards Bay coal:** $112/t vs $69 a year ago.
- **Thungela's H1 prices** were realised 13–16% *below* benchmark, mostly because of old fixed-price contracts at
  Ensham. Management expects that gap to narrow in H2, so H2 realised prices should step up even if coal is flat.

**Balance sheet as a macro hedge.** R6.1bn net cash (~32% of market value) means:
- no refinancing risk in a hiking cycle;
- the dividend (R5.50 interim, above its 30% free-cash-flow floor) is funded.
At ~5× annualised H1 EBITDA it prices in a lot of coal pessimism.

**Risks I'm taking.**
- **Rand:** GBP/ZAR is 21.9, and rand weakness cuts sterling returns.
- **Transnet's rail:** the Eskom outage on 10–11 Oct lost 174kt of exports.
- **Coal price:** a ceasefire in the Middle East would deflate energy, and coal with it.
- MAC-2 (halve energy if Brent breaks its 50-day average) will cover this position too.

**Sizing.**
- 5% of equity (~£500) from the £2,010 cash balance, so MAC-1's 20% money-market floor stays intact.
- No stamp duty (South African-incorporated). No FX charge (sterling line).
